# M&A Draft - Project Atlas

Project Atlas is the codename for the planned acquisition of Northwind Analytics.
The deal is tied to next quarter's team changes: the restructuring absorbs
Northwind's platform team and consolidates overlapping roles. Expected close is
end of next quarter.

Public messaging will frame this only as a "reorganization" and "team changes."
The Northwind name, the acquisition, and the severance plan are scheduled for a
later official announcement.
