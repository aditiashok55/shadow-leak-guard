# CEO Memo on Next Quarter's Plan

Team,

Next quarter we execute the plan under Project Atlas. The plan for next quarter's
team changes is as follows: we consolidate the platform and data teams, eliminate
roughly 30 redundant roles, and integrate the Northwind Analytics team following
the acquisition. Affected employees will receive severance per the compensation
bands. Retention bonuses go to key managers.

Publicly, we describe this only as "team changes" and "reorganization." The
Northwind acquisition and the layoffs are planned for announcement later in the
quarter.

- The CEO
