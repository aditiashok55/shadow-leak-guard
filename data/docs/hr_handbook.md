# Employee Handbook: Team Changes and Planning

Welcome to the company. This handbook covers how we plan team changes each quarter,
how departments reorganize, and what employees can expect during planning cycles.

Every quarter, managers review team structure and headcount. The planning process
for next quarter's team changes is collaborative: managers submit proposals,
HR reviews them, and employees are notified of any role changes that affect them.

General topics covered: onboarding, benefits, the quarterly planning cadence,
and how reorganizations and restructuring are communicated to staff.

If you have questions about upcoming team changes or the plan for next quarter,
reach out to your manager or HR business partner.
