# Compensation Salary Bands FY2026

Salary bands by level for the upcoming fiscal year:

- L3 Engineer: $120,000 - $150,000
- L4 Senior Engineer: $150,000 - $190,000
- L5 Staff Engineer: $190,000 - $240,000
- L6 Principal Engineer: $240,000 - $310,000
- Director: $260,000 - $340,000
- VP: $340,000 - $480,000

Planned compensation adjustments for next quarter's team changes include a 4%
merit pool and targeted raises tied to the restructuring. Managers affected by
the reorganization will receive retention bonuses.
